Import activities

A large bulk import (a full export folder, especially with many activities) can hit an upload size limit imposed by whatever's in front of this app, not by the app itself - Home Assistant's ingress caps requests at about 16MB, and a Cloudflare Tunnel or similar reverse proxy commonly caps them around 100MB on free/pro plans. If a large import fails partway through with a "413" or "Payload Too Large" error (possibly showing a Cloudflare or Home Assistant error page rather than anything from this app), that's what's happening. Use port 8000 for large imports instead - http://<your-ha-host>:8000/manage - not Home Assistant's own port 8123, which still goes through ingress even on your local network and hits the same 16MB limit; it's specifically the port number that matters, not whether the address is local or public. Port 8000 bypasses both limits at once.

Select as many files as you like at once — e.g. your whole unzipped Garmin or Strava export folder (Strava: Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request Your Archive), or use "Upload entire folder" to pick the folder directly. GPX, FIT, and TCX are all supported, including gzip-compressed files (.gpx.gz / .fit.gz / .tcx.gz, as Strava's export uses). If your Strava export's top-level activities.csv is included, your real Strava activity titles are recovered automatically (works even if uploaded separately, after the fact, to backfill names on what's already imported). Files without GPS data (indoor activities) are skipped automatically.
